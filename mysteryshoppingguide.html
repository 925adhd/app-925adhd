Is This For Me?
Requirements + ADHD compatibility
2 min ▼

✅ You'll Love It If...

  • You have reliable transportation (car preferred)
  • You can act natural while secretly evaluating
  • You notice details others miss (ADHD superpower!)
  • You can write clear, detailed reports
  • You have $100-200 for upfront purchases (reimbursed)

❌ Skip It If...

  • No reliable transportation
  • Can't afford upfront spending (wait 30-45 days for reimbursement)
  • Hate writing detailed reports
  • Can't visit locations during business hours
  • Struggle with deadlines (shops have strict due dates)

💜 ADHD Perspective

Why it works: Every shop is different! Novelty, real-world tasks, immediate purpose, and you get to play detective.

Watch out: Report writing can be tedious. Set reminders for deadlines. Don't overcommit.

ADHD Success Tips
Make this work for your brain
3 min ▼

⚡ Use Your ADHD Superpowers

  • Detail radar: You notice things others miss - perfect for evaluations
  • Novelty seeking: Each shop is a new adventure
  • Improv skills: ADHD brains are often great at thinking on their feet

📱 Essential Tools

  • Voice recorder app: Record notes immediately after leaving
  • Calendar with alerts: Never miss a deadline
  • Mileage tracker: MileIQ for tax deductions
  • Receipt photo app: Snap every receipt immediately

⏰ Report Writing Hack

The report is the hard part for ADHD brains. Here's the trick:

  1. Do the shop
  2. Voice-record your observations in the car
  3. Write the report SAME DAY (don't wait!)
  4. Set a 30-min timer - makes it a game

⚠️ Pitfall Prevention

  • Don't overbook: Start with 2-3 shops/week max
  • Check deadlines: Missing them = bad reputation
  • Budget for reimbursement lag: 30-45 day wait is normal
